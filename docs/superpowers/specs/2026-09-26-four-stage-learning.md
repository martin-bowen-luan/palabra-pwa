# Four-stage English learning

English home separates new learning and due review; each group contains at most ten distinct words. Each whole group completes choice, contextual recall, unaided recall, then spelling. Existing Spanish and unfinished legacy sessions remain resumable. Wrong answers repeat immediately and, after correction, return after three other answers. Spelling also offers skip, which is persisted and schedules an elevated-priority review rather than marking the word fluent.

English spacing uses product intervals of 10 minutes, 1, 2, 4, 7, 15, 30 days. These are an expanding-spacing heuristic, not a scientifically universal Ebbinghaus timetable. Review priority combines elapsed/scheduled interval with a modest skipped-word boost. Successful delayed review advances one interval; failure or skip resets to the short interval. No scheduling advancement for repeated successes within the same group. Fluent words remain excluded. The due count refreshes while the app is open.

Choice feedback compares the selected word and definition with the target. Spelling feedback aligns the submitted and accepted spelling, marking missing, extra and replaced letters. Recall requires checking the revealed answer before reporting success. Audio replay remains available. Rounds, feedback, skips and progress are durably stored with existing atomic/revision checks.

Word detail exposes selectable derived-word, root, synonym and irregular-form tabs. Use source-backed data only; unavailable relationships have an explicit empty state. Derivatives are distinct from inflection. Preserve offline operation and existing source links.

Visual direction: practice paper #F5F6F2, ink #172128, annotation blue #3157C8, rules #D8DDE1, success #247461, error #B84C49. Source Serif 4 for words, Noto Sans SC for controls. Home has two clear study actions; four actual rounds use numbered progress, not decorative labels. Mobile-first, 44px targets, focus, reduced motion and dark theme.
